One funny, true one-line review of the most boring everyday object we
could name: a paperclip. The laugh comes from a real, recognisable
detail of the object — a paperclip's whole life is holding other
people's papers together, here four years of tax paperwork, while
staying exactly itself — and the last words sharpen it: it is the only
thing in this office with its life in order.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(117 characters, 118 including the trailing newline — inside the
gate's 1–200 character limit, single line).
